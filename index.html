附錄一　Sample of Non-Competition Clause
勞動基準法第9-1條規定競業禁止四要件（缺一無效）：
① 雇主有應受保護之正當營業利益　② 勞工能接觸或使用營業秘密
③ 競業禁止期間/區域/範圍未逾合理範疇　④ 雇主對勞工有合理補償
違反者約定無效；離職後最長不得逾二年，超過者縮短為二年。
1. Non-Competition.

(a) During Employment.

Except as described in paragraph (c) below, during Employee's employment hereunder, Employee shall not engage, directly or indirectly, as an employee, officer, director, partner, manager, consultant, agent, owner (other than a minority shareholder or other equity interest of not more than 1% of a company whose equity interests are publicly traded on a nationally recognized stock exchange or over-the-counter) or in any other capacity, in any competition with the Company or any of its subsidiaries.

§1(a) 受雇期間競業禁止
受雇期間全面適用，禁止以任何身份從事與本公司及其所有子公司相競爭之業務。
Owner 的例外：非上市公司持股 <50%；上市公司持股 <1%（注意：上市公司門檻更嚴格）

(b) Subsequent to Employment.

Except as described in paragraph (c) below, for a two year period following the termination of Employee's employment for any reason or without reason, Employee shall not in any capacity … directly or indirectly advise, manage, render or perform services to or for any person or entity which is engaged in a business competitive to that of the Company or any of its subsidiaries … within any geographical location wherein the Company or any of its subsidiaries produces, sells or markets its goods and services at the time of such termination or within a one-year period prior to such termination.

§1(b) 離職後——兩個關鍵時間點
① 競業禁止期間：離職後 兩年
② 地理範圍：以公司在 離職時 或 離職前一年內 曾有業務的任何地點（向前追溯一年）

(c) Notwithstanding paragraphs (a) and (b) above, the Company acknowledges that the activities included in ________ [insert items or related Exhibits] hereto will not be prohibited by paragraphs (a) and (b) above. In addition, the Company agrees that any future stock option agreement will include a similar exception from the non-competition provisions.

§1(c) 例外條款——員工的談判空間
員工可與公司談判，將「不受禁止的行為」列入附件，增加競業禁止限制範圍合理的機會。
股票選擇權（stock option）在本合約中作為競業禁止的補償工具， future stock option agreement 須包含 similar to（非 identical）的例外範圍。
2. Non-solicitation.

For a two year period following the termination of Employee's employment for any reason or without reason, Employee shall not solicit or induce any person who was an employee of the Company or any of its subsidiaries on the date of Employee's termination or within three months prior to leaving her employment with the Company … to leave their employment with the Company.

§2 禁止挖角（Non-Solicitation）
期間：離職後 兩年
保護範圍：離職前 三個月內 仍在職的員工
行為：solicit（招攬）or induce（誘使）sb. to leave their employment（離職）
3. Return of Documents.

Immediately upon termination of employment, Employee will return to the Company … all the Company's or any of its subsidiaries' papers, documents and things, including information stored for use in or with computers and software … (and all copies thereof) … regardless whether such papers, documents or things contain Confidential Information or Trade Secrets.

歸還義務不限於含有機密資訊的文件——所有公司文件（含電腦檔案）一律須歸還， 並以書面形式證明已歸還。
4. No Conflicts.

To the extent that they exist, Employee will not disclose to the Company … any of Employee's previous employer's confidential information or trade secrets. Further, Employee represents and warrants that Employee has not previously assumed any obligations inconsistent with those of this Agreement and that employment by the Company does not conflict with any prior obligations to third parties. … the Company may forward a copy of this Article … to any future prospective or actual employer, and the Employee releases the Company from any claimed liability …

§4 無義務衝突三大重點
① 不洩漏前公司機密 → 避免現任公司違反營業秘密法的法人責任
② 聲明與保證（represents and warrants）本合約不與前公司義務衝突
③ 公司可告知受僱人未來潛在雇主；受僱人不得因此向公司求償
5. Agreement on Fairness.

Employee acknowledges that: (i) this Agreement has been specifically bargained between the parties and reviewed by Employee, (ii) Employee has had an opportunity to obtain legal counsel to review this Agreement, and (iii) the covenants made by and duties imposed upon Employee hereby are fair, reasonable and minimally necessary to protect the legitimate business interests of the Company, and such covenants and duties will not place an undue burden upon Employee's livelihood in the event of termination of Employee's employment by the Company and the strict enforcement of the covenants contained herein.

§5 不是萬靈丹！
本條只是員工承認合約已公平協議，但競業禁止是否真的有效， 仍須看：① 競業禁止時間/範圍是否合理 ② 代償金額是否實質足夠。
光有這條並不能保證合約完全有效。
6. Equitable Relief and Remedies.

Employee acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement will cause substantial and irreparable harm to the Company for which money damages would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, the Company shall in any such event be entitled to seek injunctive and other forms of equitable relief … and the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover from the other, the prevailing party's costs (including, without limitation, reasonable attorneys' fees) … in addition to any other rights or remedies available at law, in equity, by statute or ________ [insert related articles or agreements].

§6 衡平救濟（Equitable Relief）
• in addition to and not in lieu of：禁制令是附加救濟，不取代其他 rights and remedy
• reasonable attorneys' fees：必須在合約中事前約定，否則台灣法院通常不納入損害賠償範圍
• at law / in equity / by statute / by agreement：四種救濟來源並列
附錄二　Sample of Nondisclosure Agreement
NDA 合約架構概覽
從前言定義「揭露方/接收方」可知：此為雙方（Mutual）NDA。
公司制式 NDA 常分三版：NDA_Disclosing / NDA_Receiving / NDA_Mutual
前言（Preamble）

This Nondisclosure Agreement (hereinafter as "Agreement") is made and entered into as of ○○○○ [insert date] (the "Effective Date") between ○○○ [insert names] and ○○○ [insert names] (such party when disclosing information being the "Disclosing Party" and when receiving such information being the "Receiving Party").

as of [date] = 從___日期開始 ＝ As at / on（生效時間點）
made and entered into = 訂立（合約締結的標準用語）
hereinafter as = 以下簡稱
Effective Date = 生效日
1. Purpose

The parties wish to explore a business opportunity of mutual interest relating to ○○○ [insert items or names] (hereinafter as " Proposed Transaction"), and in connection with this opportunity, each party has and will disclose to the other certain confidential technical and business information which the Disclosing Party desires the Receiving Party to treat as confidential.

「has and will disclose」翻譯陷阱
× 「須揭露一定程度機密技術與業務內容」（太過概略，未展現時間性）
✓ 「任一方已向且未來將向對方揭露若干機密技術資訊及商業資訊」
2. Definitions

(1) "Confidential Information" means information furnished by the Disclosing Party or its Representatives to the Receiving Party or its Representatives, that is designated as "Confidential" or " Proprietary" by stamp or legend if communicated in writing or other tangible form or otherwise orally at the time of disclosure with a written confirmation within ○○ [insert numbers] days describing the Confidential Information communicated orally.

Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to, past, present and future business, financial and commercial information, business concepts, prices and pricing methods, marketing and customer information, financial forecasts and projections, technical data and information, formulae, analyses, trade secrets, ideas, methods, processes, know-how, computer programs, products, equipment, product road maps, prototypes, samples, designs, data sheets, schematics, configurations, specifications, techniques, drawings, and any other data or information.

Confidential Information does not include information which:

  1. was generally available to the public prior to the time of disclosure by the Disclosing Party;
  2. becomes generally available to the public after disclosure by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party through no action or inaction of the Receiving Party;
  3. was already in the possession of the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis prior to its disclosure by the Disclosing Party … as shown by the Receiving Party's files and records immediately prior to the time of disclosure;
  4. becomes available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis from a person other than the Disclosing Party … who is not otherwise bound by a confidentiality agreement …; or
  5. is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, as shown by the Receiving Party's files and records immediately prior to the time of disclosure.
例外(c)(e) 的舉證要求
都需要有「揭露前的書面檔案紀錄（files and records immediately prior to disclosure）」作為佐證， 缺少書面紀錄則無法援引此例外！

(2) "Person", whether or not capitalized, shall be broadly interpreted to include, without limitation, any corporation, company, partnership, limited liability company, other entity or individual.

(3) "Representative" means, as to any person, that person's directors, officers, employees, agents and advisors.

Confidential（機密）/ Proprietary（專有）＝ 兩種標記方式
know-how = 專門技術　product road maps = 產品路徑圖
data（資料）≠ information（資訊）
capitalized = 首字大寫（× 資本化）
on a non-confidential basis = 在不負保密義務的情形下
3. Non-Use and Non-Disclosure

The Receiving Party (1) shall not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than in connection with its evaluation of the Proposed Transaction or the consummation of the Proposed Transaction; and (2) except as required by law, shall keep all Confidential Information confidential, and shall not disclose or reveal any Confidential Information to any person other than its Representatives who otherwise need to know the Confidential Information for evaluation of the Proposed Transaction or the consummation of the Proposed Transaction, provided that the Receiving Party shall cause those persons to observe the terms of this Agreement. The Receiving Party shall be responsible for any breach of the terms of this Agreement by it or its Representatives.

§3 三大重點
① 目的限制：機密資訊只能用於評估或完成計畫交易
② need to know 原則：只揭露給必須知悉的代表人（法律要求除外→見§5）
③ 接收方對代表人的違約行為負責
4. Maintenance of Confidentiality

Receiving Party shall use at least the same degree of care, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care, to protect the secrecy of and avoid disclosure and unauthorized use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information as Receiving Party employs to protect its own most highly confidential information.

§4 雙層注意義務標準
上限：與保護自己最高機密一樣的注意義務
下限（最低標準）：reasonable degree of care（合理注意程度）——任何情況均不得低於此
義務核心：避免「disclosure（揭露）」AND「unauthorized use（未經授權使用）」——兩者並重
5. Mandatory Disclosure

In the event that the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives are requested pursuant to, or required by, applicable law or regulation or by legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall provide the Disclosing Party with prompt notice of such request or requirement in order to enable the Disclosing Party to seek an appropriate protective order or other remedy. In the event that such protective order … is not obtained, the Receiving Party or its Representative shall disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information which is legally required to be disclosed and use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that all Confidential Information that is so disclosed will be accorded confidential treatment.

§5 強制揭露三步驟
① 立即通知揭露方（讓揭露方聲請保護令）
② 最小揭露原則（只揭露法律所要求的最小程度）
③ use commercially reasonable efforts 保障仍受保密處理
6. No Obligation

Nothing herein shall obligate any party to proceed with any transaction between them, and each party reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to terminate the discussions contemplated by this Agreement.

第6、7、9條構成一組——NDA 的三個「不」
本約只是「評估交易機會」，揭露資訊後：
§ 6：不必然繼續進行交易（No Obligation）
§ 7：不擔保資訊正確性（No Warranty）
§ 9：不授予任何 IP 權利（No License）
7. No Warranty

All Confidential Information is provided "as is." Each party hereto acknowledges that … neither it nor its Representatives … makes any warranties, express, implied or otherwise, regarding its accuracy, completeness or performance.

8. Return of Materials

Confidential Information shall be and remain the property of the Disclosing Party or its licensors, as the case may be. The Receiving Party shall … promptly deliver … all Confidential Information, and, at the Receiving Party's election, return or destroy … all copies, reproductions, summaries, analyses or extracts thereof or based thereon (whether in hard-copy form or on intangible media, such as electronic mail or computer files) …

shall be and remain = 應是（為）且維持是（為）
as the case may be = 視情況
its licensors = 其授權人
at the Receiving Party's election = 由接收方決定（返還或銷毀）
extracts thereof = 從其中之摘錄（thereof = of it）
based thereon = 以其為基礎（thereon = on it）
hard-copy = 有紙本形式（相對於 soft copy）
9. No License

Neither this Agreement nor the furnishing of any information shall be construed to transfer or grant (either expressly or by implication, estoppel or otherwise) to any party any license, right to use, or other rights under any invention, copyright, patent, trade secret, trademark or other intellectual property right of another party.

No License ≠ 無許可證（×）
正確理解：揭露資訊 不構成 向另一方轉讓或授予任何智慧財產權利， 無論是明示（expressly）、默示（by implication）或禁反言（estoppel）均不構成授權。
10. Term

The Receiving Party will hold Confidential Information in confidence for a period of ○○ [insert numbers] years after the Effective Date. The term of this Agreement shall be ○○ [insert numbers] years from the Effective Date, unless earlier terminated by either party upon providing ○○ [insert numbers] days prior written notice. However, the expiration of this Agreement shall not relieve the parties of their obligations hereunder regarding the protection and use of Confidential Information during the periods set forth above.

合約期間（Term）≠ 保密期間（Confidentiality Period）
兩者通常不同——保密期間通常更長。
合約到期後，在保密期間屆滿前，保密義務仍然存在！
11. Disputes and Remedies

All disputes … which the parties cannot settle amicably within ○○ [insert numbers] months after written notification by one party to the other or such longer period as may be mutually agreed upon, shall be finally settled by arbitration in ○○ [insert country] … by ○○ [insert numbers] arbitrators … The arbitration proceedings and all communications related thereto shall be conducted in English. Any decision resulting from such arbitration shall be final and binding upon the parties. … The parties hereby waive any and all rights to a judicial review of the arbitrator's decision and any award included therein. … either party may obtain preliminary or temporary injunctive relief at any time from a court of competent jurisdiction in addition to and not in lieu of any other rights and remedies including monetary damages …

§11 仲裁 + 禁制令
• 仲裁裁決（award）：拘束（binding）雙方；雙方放棄司法複審
• award = 司法/仲裁裁決（× 獎賞）；binding = 拘束（× 綁定）
• 臨時禁制令 → 可直接向法院聲請（不用等仲裁）；永久禁制令 → 須仲裁
• in addition to and not in lieu of：附加，不取代其他救濟
台灣對照：
preliminary injunction ↔ 智審法第52條「聲請定暫時狀態假處分」
permanent injunction ↔ 營業秘密法第11條「排除侵害請求權」
12. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of ○○○ [insert country], without regard to its conflicts of laws rules.

governed by = 以…為準據法
conflicts of law = 衝突法 / 國際私法（不是指「互相衝突的法律」）
法院 → 管轄（jurisdiction）；法律 → 準據法（governing law）
13. Notices

Any notice related to this Agreement shall be in writing effective upon receipt … when received in person, by certified mail return receipt, private overnight courier service requiring signature upon receipt or via email … The parties do not need to comply with this section to mark information as Confidential Information.

notice = 通知（× 注意事項）
certified mail return receipt = 雙掛號
重點：將資訊標記為機密資訊時，不適用本節規定——交付資訊時直接 mark「機密」即可， 不需要透過這個繁瑣的正式通知方式。
14. Miscellaneous

… no failure or delay by any party hereto in exercising any right, power or privilege hereunder shall operate as a waiver thereof … This Agreement is personal to the parties and non-assignable without the prior written consent of all other parties. This Agreement shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties hereto and their successors and assigns.

15. Entire Agreement

This Agreement contains the entire Agreement between the parties hereto … and it supersedes all prior or contemporaneous written or oral agreements thereon. No modification … shall be binding … unless approved in writing by each such party. This Agreement may be executed in a number of counterparts with the same effect as if all signatories had signed the same document. Fax or electronic signatures on this Agreement shall be fully binding and effective for all purposes.

Entire Agreement ≠ 總結（×）
正確：完整協議 / 全部合約條款 / 完整合意條款
counterparts = 副本分開簽署（× 多份正本）
binding = 拘束（× 綁定）

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have caused this Nondisclosure Agreement to be duly executed by their authorized representatives.

附錄三　競業禁止約定書
本中文版約定書與英文版 Non-Competition Clause（附錄一）對照閱讀。
依台灣勞基法第9-1條：違反四要件之一者約定無效；離職後最長不逾二年。

立約定書人________________（甲方）
               ________________（乙方）

緣○○○（下稱乙方）受○○○（下稱甲方）聘僱擔任之○○○之職務，今雙方就乙方應對甲方負競業禁止義務一事達成合意，爰訂定以下條款（下稱本合約）以資遵循：

第一條（競業禁止範圍）

乙方同意於 受僱於甲方之期間內、或雙方僱傭契約終止或解除後24個月內， 於中華民國境內，非經甲方事前書面同意，不得為下列行為：

  1. 以自己或他人名義經營與甲方相同或類似之事業、或投資前述事業達該事業資本額或已發行股份總額數 百分之五 以上。
  2. 為與甲方業務相同或類似之公司、商號或個人之 受僱人、受任人、承攬人或顧問； 或為此類單位招徠甲方之現職員工前往任職。
  3. 乙方不得直接或間接從事任何與任職於甲方時所負責之業務，或與該業務有關之事務相競爭之行為，包括但不限於提升、改善或調整甲方競爭者競爭力或業務經營之行為，為甲方競爭者提供勞務，接觸、拜訪甲方客戶（含洽商中之客戶）或向甲方客戶要約、銷售相同或相類甲方之產品或服務，或向甲方之競爭者銷售或授權與甲方研發成果相同或相類之 智慧財產權。
第二條（競業禁止對償）

乙方同意甲方於在職期間內支付乙方之薪資或獎勵，包括但不限於紅利、年終獎金等，均已包含要求乙方負擔本合約所定競業禁止...

競業禁止「對償」注意！
勞基法第9-1條第4款：合理補償不包括勞工於工作期間所受領之給付（在職薪資不算）。
最好在入職或升遷時，另外明確約定離職後的競業禁止補償金額。
